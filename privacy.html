Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how XENIA Personal Assistant ("XENIA") handles information when its owner connects a Google account for the YouTube integration.

1. Information accessed

After the account owner authorizes access through Google OAuth, XENIA may access YouTube channel information, video information and YouTube Analytics data permitted by the permissions shown during Google's consent process. The integration is intended for read-only reporting. It does not upload, modify or delete YouTube videos, comments or channel settings.

2. How information is used

Information obtained from Google APIs is used to provide the owner with requested channel reports, statistics, summaries and related assistant responses. XENIA does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling or selling data.

3. Storage and processing

XENIA runs on the owner's computer using a self-hosted n8n installation. OAuth credentials are handled and stored by that installation. Retrieved information may appear in local workflow execution records or other local project storage, depending on the owner's n8n configuration and retention settings.

An HTTPS tunnel may relay the Google authorization callback to the local n8n instance. Reports requested by the owner may be sent through Telegram to the selected chat.

4. Sharing

XENIA does not sell Google user data or share it with data brokers. Data is transmitted to Google when using Google authorization and YouTube APIs. Information sent through Telegram is handled by Telegram under its own terms and privacy policy. The configured tunnel provider may relay the OAuth callback.

5. Data retention and deletion

Information is retained on the operator-controlled system only as long as needed, subject to local n8n execution-history and storage settings. The owner can remove stored workflow data and revoke XENIA's Google access. Google access can be revoked from Google Account security settings. Removing local credentials from n8n prevents further use of those credentials.

6. Security

Security depends on the computer, operating system, n8n configuration, account access and configured network services. The operator should protect the system and credentials and avoid sharing access to n8n or local files.

7. Children's privacy

XENIA is a personal assistant for its owner and is not designed as a service for children.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the project's functionality or data practices change. The effective date will be revised when an update is published.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to skeytman@gmail.com.

This policy describes the intended personal, self-hosted use of XENIA. It must be updated if actual data flows, permissions, storage or third-party services change.
