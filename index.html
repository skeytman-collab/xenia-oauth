XENIA

Personal Assistant

XENIA is a personal assistant operated by its owner on a privately controlled computer. It connects selected services to help with everyday tasks through a Telegram interface.

YouTube integration

After the owner authorizes access through Google OAuth, XENIA can retrieve YouTube channel information and read-only analytics to help review channel performance, video statistics and reports. The integration is not intended to publish, edit or delete YouTube content.

How it works

XENIA runs on the owner's computer using a self-hosted n8n installation. Google authorization and API requests are handled through Google's services. Reports may be delivered to the owner's Telegram chat when requested.

Privacy

XENIA is a personal project, not a public service. Google account access is granted only after the account owner completes Google's authorization process.
